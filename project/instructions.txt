
Explore the rooms, collect useful items, and make eco-friendly choices.

Game commands:
- move
- collect
- eco
- save
- load
- inventory
- menu

You need the Key and Shoes to leave the house.

Other items are optional and may be useful depending on your activity.
Once you collect an item, it cannot be removed from your inventory.
Choose carefully before collecting an item, because your choices can affect your final score.

Scoring:
- Base score: 60 points
- Each eco action: +10 points

Jogging:
- Laptop collected: -10 points
- Phone collected: -5 points

Work:
- Phone missing: -10 points
- Laptop missing: -5 points

Return to the Hall whenever you want to check if you are ready to leave.

Good luck!
